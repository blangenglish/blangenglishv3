Thesis: Students can build a meaningful invitation by moving from places, to details, to speaking, to writing.
Audience: Adult English learners, A1-A2.
Arc: Notice language -> personalize places -> rehearse aloud -> write a real-life email -> reflect.
Slides:
1. Invite a friend by describing real places and asking useful questions.
2. Guess a place by connecting adjectives, actions, and context.
3. Choose five personally meaningful places.
4. Make each place vivid with contrast and action.
5. Rehearse two places with a partner before writing.
6. Turn spoken ideas into a friendly invitation email.
7. Share and listen for a favorite place.
8. Check meaning through description, action, and questions.
9. Reflect on what felt easy and difficult.
